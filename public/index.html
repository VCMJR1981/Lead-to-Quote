// Questions

Answers, straight up.

Real ones. Not the marketing kind.

Do I need to be tech-savvy? +
If you can text, you can use this. Setup is 2 minutes. No training, no manuals, no calls with a "success rep."
What happens after my 5 free quotes? +
You'll be asked to subscribe for $29/month. If you don't, your account pauses — your data stays safe, you just can't send new quotes until you subscribe.
How do Stripe deposits work? +
Connect your Stripe account once (3 minutes). Set the deposit percentage per quote. Customer pays through the quote link with card or Apple Pay. Money goes straight to your bank — we never touch it.
Who pays the Stripe fees? +
Your choice. Pass them to the customer as a processing fee, or absorb them yourself. Toggle per quote or set a default in settings.
What if a customer wants to pay cash? +
Cash works fine. Payment is optional on every quote. Some customers pay through the link, some don't. Up to you and them.
My trade isn't listed yet. Can I still use it? +
Yes. The product works for any trade — we just haven't built a dedicated landing page yet. Start free, pick the closest trade during setup, and you can customize templates from there.
Is there a contract? +
No. Monthly billing, cancel in one click from settings. No phone calls, no retention pitch.
